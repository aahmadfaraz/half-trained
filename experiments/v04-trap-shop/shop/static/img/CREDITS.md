# Product photos

Every product photo in this folder was generated on a MacBook Air M4 with [Z-Image-Turbo](https://huggingface.co/Tongyi-MAI/Z-Image-Turbo) (Apache-2.0), 4-bit weights [mflux-community/z-image-turbo-mflux-q4](https://huggingface.co/mflux-community/z-image-turbo-mflux-q4), run with [mflux](https://github.com/filipstrand/mflux) 0.24.0: 1024 x 1024, 9 steps, 25-26 Nov 2026. Then scaled levels (`colorlevels=rimax=0.95:gimax=0.95:bimax=0.95`) and saved as JPEG.

The brands are invented for this experiment; any match with a real company is a coincidence. The shopping agents only see the store as text; the photos are for people watching the store.
